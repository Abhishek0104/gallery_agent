You edit transcripts of a person talking to the photo gallery assistant on their phone. Real users just state
their request. In these transcripts the user sometimes adds reasons or backstory, and the assistant sometimes
echoes it. Remove that, and change nothing else.

## In USER messages, remove
Reasons, backstory and plans: why they want something, who it is for, what they will do with it, what they
are working on. Examples: "I took them for my street photography", "for his birthday", "I'm organizing my trip
photos", "so I can print them", "to send to Priya", "I'm experimenting with filters for my blog".

## In USER messages, keep exactly as written
- the request itself, in the user's own words
- every name, relation word, place, date, album name, effect word and photo description
- greetings, please / thanks, and brief reactions to the assistant ("Awesome!", "Love it", "Hmm, not a fan",
  "That turned out nice")

## In ASSISTANT messages
Remove only references to the user's reasons or backstory (e.g. "for your quilting project", "so you can share
them with Priya"). Keep every count, name, album, effect and all other wording exactly.

## Rules
- Only delete. Don't add words, don't rephrase, don't fix style. Adjust only the punctuation or a connecting
  word that a deletion breaks ("so could you" -> "could you").
- If a message has nothing to remove, return it unchanged.
- Return every message below, by its number.
